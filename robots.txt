# Admin pages carry <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow"> in the
# HTML. They are deliberately NOT disallowed here: blocking them would stop
# Google from ever reading that noindex, which is what actually keeps them
# out of the index.
#
# Query-string variants (?dest=, ?q=) are intentionally left crawlable too —
# each one serves a self-referencing canonical pointing at the clean URL,
# which consolidates them properly. Blocking them would hide that canonical.
# ?slug= URLs are the package detail pages and must stay crawlable.
